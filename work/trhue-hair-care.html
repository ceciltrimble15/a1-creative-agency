TRHUE Hair Care

A/1 Creative built a premium website and brand system for this Cincinnati, Ohio hair care studio, giving new clients a clear way to see the work and book.

The need

TRHUE needed a credible, premium online presence that matched the quality of the in-studio experience and made it easy for new clients to see the work and book.

Systems we built

Website

A premium, mobile-first site built to match the quality of the in-studio experience.

Brand & visual identity

A cohesive visual identity carried across the site and client touchpoints.

Service menu / product presentation

Clear presentation of services and products for prospective clients.

Booking infrastructure

A straightforward path for new clients to book directly from the site.

Lead capture

A system for collecting and organizing new client inquiries.
