Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

A1 Creative Agency ("A1 Creative," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit a1creativeagency.com, contact us, or use our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

When you submit a form, call, or text us, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • The service you are interested in and any preferred date
  • Any details or message you choose to share

Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, our hosting and analytics providers may log standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages viewed, in order to operate and secure the site.

Call and text message information

If you call us or we attempt to reach you, our telephony provider processes your phone number and call/message metadata to deliver our missed-call recovery and follow-up service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide the services you request
  • To send a text message back when we miss your call, so we can help you faster
  • To capture, organize, and follow up on leads and appointments
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. SMS / Text Messaging

By providing your phone number or by calling us, you consent to receive text messages from A1 Creative related to your inquiry, including automated missed-call responses and follow-up about your request. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance. Opting out of SMS will not affect your ability to contact us by phone or email.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate, such as our messaging/telephony provider, lead and records storage, email delivery, and website hosting — solely to perform services for us.
  • Legal and safety recipients when required by law, to enforce our agreements, or to protect rights, safety, and property.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide our services, respond to your inquiry, and meet legal, accounting, or reporting requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to opt out of certain uses. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Third-Party Links

Our site may link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page, with an updated "Last updated" date.
