SMS / Text Messaging Program

When you provide your mobile phone number to A1 Creative Agency — for example, by submitting a contact or quote form, replying to a missed call, or otherwise opting in — you consent to receive recurring SMS/MMS text messages from us related to your inquiry, appointments, lead follow-up, customer care, account updates, and service notifications.

SMS opt-in: Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase. You opt in by giving us your mobile number through a form, by texting us first, or by checking a consent box where provided.

  • Message frequency varies based on your interaction with us.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Charges are set by your mobile carrier.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out and stop receiving messages.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at admin@a1creativeagency.com or (513) 440-3329.
  • Supported carriers may include AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, and others. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your mobile information is never sold. Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information collected automatically when you use our website:

  • Contact details — name, business name, email address, and phone/mobile number you submit through our forms or calls.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide quotes, services, and support.
  • To send SMS messages you have consented to receive, including appointment, follow-up, and service notifications.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with trusted service providers who help us operate our business (such as hosting, email, CRM, and SMS/telephony platforms), and only as needed to deliver the services you requested or as required by law. As stated above, mobile numbers and SMS opt-in consent are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Security & Retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. We retain information only for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal requirements. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.
