Privacy Policy

A/1 Creative Agency LLC · Effective July 30, 2026 · Last updated July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how A/1 Creative Agency LLC ("A/1 Creative Agency," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects the information you provide when you contact us, submit a form on a1creativeagency.com, call our business line, or take part in our SMS text-messaging program.

1. Who we are

A/1 Creative Agency LLC is a revenue-infrastructure company based in Cincinnati, Ohio. We build websites, automation, lead-capture, booking, and missed-call-recovery systems for local businesses. You can reach us at privacy@a1creativeagency.com or (513) 440-3329.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details you submit — name, business name, mobile phone number, and email address.
  • Inquiry details — the service you're interested in, preferred dates, and any message or business-assessment answers you provide.
  • Consent records — when and where you opted in to text messaging, the exact consent language shown to you, and technical audit data (timestamp and IP address) used to prove your consent.
  • Call information — if you call our business line, we may record the caller number and call outcome to follow up on missed calls.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry, prepare a quote, and schedule calls or appointments.
  • To send you the text messages you asked to receive (see the SMS section below).
  • To follow up on missed calls and leads so your request isn't lost.
  • To operate, secure, and improve our services and internal systems.
  • To meet legal, tax, and regulatory obligations.

4. SMS / text-messaging program & mobile information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your mobile phone number or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

When you check the SMS consent box on our form, or otherwise give express written consent, you agree to receive text messages from A/1 Creative Agency related to your inquiry — including quote and appointment updates, lead follow-up, missed-call recovery, and account or service notifications — at the mobile number you provide.

  • Message frequency varies based on your interaction with us.
  • Message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier.
  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. After you send STOP, we will send one confirmation message and then stop texting you.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us at privacy@a1creativeagency.com or (513) 440-3329.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared with service providers (subcontractors) strictly to help us deliver the messaging and support services above — for example, our messaging platform. All the above categories exclude text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

5. How we share information

We share information only with vendors that help us run our business — such as our messaging provider (Twilio), our database/CRM (Airtable), our website host, and our email provider — and only as needed to provide the services you requested. These vendors are bound to protect your information and use it only to perform services for us. We may also disclose information when required by law. We never sell your data.

6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal and record-keeping requirements, including retaining SMS consent records for as long as required to demonstrate compliance. You may request deletion at any time (see below).

7. Your choices & rights

  • Opt out of texts anytime by replying STOP.
  • Unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email, or by contacting us.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by emailing privacy@a1creativeagency.com.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep your data protected.

9. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for business owners and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent version, and the current version always governs.
